Assumptions & equation used

This simple simulator assumes:

  • Closed system (no parent/daughter gain or loss after formation).
  • Basic mode: initial daughter atoms = 0 (clock starts with all parent).
  • Advanced mode: allows a non‑zero initial daughter fraction D0.

We use the radioactive decay law:

N/N0 = (1/2)t/t1/2

Solving for time:

t = t1/2 · log2(N0/N)

In this app, N is the parent fraction remaining. In advanced mode, N0 is reduced by D0.
